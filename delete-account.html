Request Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your Credence account and all associated data. This includes your personal information, transaction history, savings and loan records, and push notification subscriptions.

Before you proceed: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. You will lose access to your savings records, loan history, and transaction history.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to us with the subject "Request Account Deletion" and include your account number so we can identify your account.

Email Us to Request Deletion

Or contact us manually:

Email: efferevetech2@gmail.com
Subject: Request Account Deletion
Include: Your account number

What Happens After You Submit

  1. We verify your identity by confirming your account number.
  2. Your account is deactivated within 5 business days.
  3. All associated data is permanently deleted from our servers, including:
    • Personal information (name, phone number, location)
    • Savings and transaction history
    • Loan records and repayment history
    • Push notification subscriptions
    • Authentication tokens
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What You Can Do Immediately

You can also remove data from your device at any time by:

  • Logging out of the app — this clears your authentication tokens and push subscription from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app — this removes all locally stored data from your device.

Note: These actions remove data from your device only. To delete your data from our servers, you must contact us using the method above.
