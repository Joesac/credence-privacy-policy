Privacy Policy — Credence Mobile App

Last updated: September 3, 2026

EfferveTech ("we", "us", "our") operates the Credence mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account credentials: Your account number and password when you log in.
  • Biometric data: If you enable biometric login, the App uses your device's built-in fingerprint or facial recognition to unlock your account. Your biometric data is never collected, transmitted, or stored by us — it remains on your device and is managed by your device's secure hardware.

Information collected automatically

  • Device push token: When you enable notifications, the App registers your device with OneSignal to deliver push notifications. This includes a device subscription ID and push token.
  • Platform information: The App transmits your device platform (Android or iOS) to our server to correctly route push notifications.

Information from your account

  • Account and transaction data: Savings balances, loan details, transaction history, and deposit/withdrawal records associated with your member account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and grant access to your account.
  • Account services: To display your savings, loans, and transaction history.
  • Push notifications: To send you real-time alerts about account activity, including deposits and withdrawals.
  • Security: To maintain and restore your session using encrypted tokens stored securely on your device.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Authentication tokens (access and refresh tokens) are stored in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave). They are never stored in plaintext.
  • Passwords are never stored on your device. Only encrypted authentication tokens are retained.
  • Biometric login uses only your stored refresh token and account number — no password or biometric data is retained by the App.
  • All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Third-Party Services

  • OneSignal: Used for push notification delivery. OneSignal receives your device push token to route notifications. See OneSignal's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used as the transport layer for Android push notifications. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained for as long as your membership with Credence is active.
  • Push notification subscription records are removed when you log out.
  • Authentication tokens on your device are removed when you log out or when they expire.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your account and transaction data within the App.
  • Notifications: You can enable or disable push notifications at any time in the App's settings.
  • Biometric login: You can use password login instead of biometrics at any time.
  • Account closure: Contact us to close your account and request deletion of your data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on this page with an updated revision date.
